Energy Cubed

Overview

Energy Cubed is a shopfront application for conveniently browsing and purchasing energy formula-related products. Customers will be able to create accounts and sign in to purchase various energy drink products. An included manager sign-in will allow for adding and updating listed items. Managers will be capable of generating reports of sales data for viewing and analyzing customer purchases. All of these features are conveniently bundled in a user-friendly interface to make utilizing the application simple and efficient.

Energy Cubed is written in C#, utilizes html for the web reports, and uses SQLite for database interactions. Also utilizes mulitithreading to increase user responsiveness.
